    Before you book

    Best season

    October to March suits most of India. Go to the hills (Himachal, Kashmir, Ladakh) from April to June, and to the Kerala hills and Karnataka after the monsoon, from September.

    Booking trains and flights

    Trains open for booking 60 days ahead on IRCTC. Popular routes sell out fast, so book early. Fly into the nearest airport shown on each place and finish the trip by road or rail.

    Permits and entry rules

    Ladakh's protected areas need an Inner Line Permit. Foreign nationals need a visa and may need Protected Area Permits. Some monuments, including the Taj Mahal, close one day a week.

    Packing

    Carry a photo ID, cotton clothes for the plains, layers for the hills, a scarf or shawl for temples, sunscreen, a reusable water bottle and a power bank.

    Health and safety

    Drink bottled or filtered water, keep basic medicines, and buy travel insurance for mountain trips. In an emergency anywhere in India, dial 112.

    Respect the place

    Remove footwear at temples and mosques, ask before photographing people, and carry your waste out of beaches, hills and villages.
